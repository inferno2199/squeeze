Questions

Can I mint more than one?

Yes, as many as you like. Every photo gives a new octopus. Each Citizen you own is its own lock in the Jar.

Is there a max supply?

No. Everyone should be able to mint their own octopus. Only the first 500 become Citizens, and that's final.

What if I mint after #500?

You still get a full Squeeze Squad NFT: the Reef, stickers, seasonal versions, badges and rarity. Only the Citizen stamp and the Jar are for the first 500.

Will the price go up?

Yes, a little: +0.05 SOL every 100 mints (#1-100 cost 0.30, #101-200 cost 0.35, and so on). The earlier you mint, the less you pay.

Can I sell it?

Yes. It's a normal Solana NFT (Metaplex Core) in a verified collection, so you can list it on NFT marketplaces. Resales pay 7.5% royalties: half to the team, half straight into the Jar.

Will my NFT ever change?

No. Your picture, name and rarity stay exactly as minted. Seasonal versions are separate pictures for sharing.

Is it safe?

You only sign one payment in your own wallet. Every mint and every Jar payout is on the blockchain for anyone to check. Admins will never DM you first or ask for your seed phrase.
